Simple Lead & Enquiry Management for Small Businesses

A lead-management routine helps a small business respond consistently and learn from genuine customer enquiries. You do not need a complicated CRM process to start: capture the enquiry, understand the request, agree on a next step and keep the record updated.

Step 1: Capture genuine enquiries in one place

Record enquiries from the channels you actually use—phone, messaging, public forms or in-person requests—when the customer has contacted you or clearly requested a response. Save the date, source, product or service requested, contact details voluntarily provided and a brief note.

Step 2: Keep records clean

Check for duplicates before adding a new record. Similar names or phone numbers may belong to different people, so verify before merging. Keep a clear status such as new, contacted, awaiting reply, completed or closed.

Step 3: Prioritise and follow up appropriately

Use the customer's stated requirement, service fit and urgency to organise next actions. Follow up only through appropriate contact methods and respect opt-outs or requests not to be contacted. Do not send indiscriminate bulk messages.

Step 4: Review outcomes

Measure the number of real enquiries, response time, completed follow-ups and outcomes. Look at source and topic trends to improve your business information and content. Separate research signals from people who actually contacted you.

Frequently asked questions

What information should I save?

Record contact details the customer voluntarily provides, their request, date, source, status and agreed next step. Collect only information needed to respond.

How can I avoid duplicate leads?

Review similar contact details and enquiry context, merge duplicates carefully and preserve the original history where useful.

How do I prioritise enquiries?

Prioritise by the customer's stated need, urgency, service fit and requested next step. Unverified signals are not confirmed leads.
